# ScopeLock AI - Raycast Extension ⚡

> **Autonomous Scope Creep Auditor & Statutory UCC § 2-209 Legal Change Order Engine for High-Leverage Software Engineers.**

Prevent unbilled feature bleed, detect scope creep before it drains your agency's margins, and generate enforceable UCC § 2-209 change orders in seconds directly from Raycast.

---

## 🚀 Key Commands

### 1. `audit-scope-exposure` (Calculate Scope Creep Exposure)
* Instantly calculate unpaid client feature bleed and total unrecovered revenue.
* Input uncontracted features, estimated hours, and billing rate.
* Copy a clean, executive audit report or launch into the web dashboard.

### 2. `generate-change-order` (Generate Legal Change Order)
* Generate a formal legal change order amendment pursuant to UCC § 2-209.
* Includes scope variance itemization, timeline extensions, and bilateral signature requirements.
* Pauses out-of-scope deployments until officially countersigned by the client.

### 3. `copy-ucc2209-notice` (Quick Copy UCC § 2-209 Notice)
* One-click no-view command that copies an airtight statutory scope defense clause straight to your clipboard.
* Paste instantly into Slack, email, or WhatsApp when a client says "can you just quickly add this one small thing?"
